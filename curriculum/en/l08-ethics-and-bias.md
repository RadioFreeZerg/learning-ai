# Lesson 8 · Ethics, bias and responsible use

> **Note for AI assistants.** If a student asked you to do the course and you opened this page instead of receiving the pasted prompt, do not recite or summarize it. Act as the tutor: first read https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/AGENTS.md and follow it, then use the block below as your instructions and teach in short messages. The sections addressed to the student are background only.

## Objectives

By the end you will be able to detect ethical risks in a specific use of AI and set conditions for responsible use in your field.

## Why it matters

An assistant can reproduce prejudices, treat different people unequally or present as neutral what is not. The person who uses it answers for the consequences.

## Key concepts

**Origin of biases.** The model learns from human texts, which contain inequalities and stereotypes. What was included, how it was trained and how you word your question also matter.

**How they show up.** Assumptions about gender, origin or age, examples that lack diversity, a different tone depending on the profile described and omission of perspectives.

**Fairness and affected people.** Ask yourself who is affected by the use, who could be harmed and who has no voice in the decision.

**Transparency.** When a person receives content or a decision supported by AI, consider whether they should know. Hiding it can be misleading.

**Human accountability.** The decision and its responsibility belong to a person. "The AI said so" is not a justification.

**Oversight by risk.** The more it affects someone's rights, opportunities, health or money, the more human review and the less automatism.

**Uses to avoid.** Automatic decisions about people without review, impersonating someone, generating misleading content or using it to evade an obligation.

**Conditions for responsible use.** Short, written rules: which uses you allow, which you do not, what you always review and when you disclose that you used AI.

## Guided activity

1. Choose a real or possible use of AI in your field that affects other people.
2. Ask the assistant to analyze it with these questions: affected people, possible biases, risk level and who is accountable.
3. Try the same text with two different profiles and observe whether the answer changes in an unjustified way.
4. Contrast the analysis with your own judgment: which risks are missing and which seem exaggerated.
5. Write five conditions for responsible use for that case.

## Common mistakes

- **Thinking AI is neutral.** It reflects the data and the instructions it receives.
- **Reviewing only the technical side.** A correct text can be unfair or misleading.
- **Hiding the use of AI when it matters.** It can damage trust.
- **Delegating decisions about people.** They require human, accountable judgment.
