# E7 · Ethics case from your field

## Purpose

Show that you can analyze a specific use of AI from an ethical standpoint and turn it into practical rules.

## Task

Choose a use case of AI in your field that affects other people. Describe it, analyze risks and possible biases (you may use the assistant for support, but you decide) and finish with conditions for responsible use. If you use a real case, anonymize names and data of third parties.

Variant for your field: use a case that already happens or could arise in your environment.

## Deliverable and format

A document of one to two pages, pasted as text, with: case, affected people, risks, test or reasoning about biases, accountable person and human review, and three to five conditions of use. Maximum 600 words. If you work with files, save it as `submissions/e07/attempt-1.md`.

## Required items

- [ ] **e7-case** · Describes a use case of AI from their field in at most 100 words.
- [ ] **e7-affected** · Identifies the affected people and at least two concrete ethical risks.
- [ ] **e7-bias** · Includes a test or reasoning about possible biases in the case.
- [ ] **e7-owner** · States who is responsible for the final decision and what human review is required.
- [ ] **e7-rules** · Writes three to five conditions for responsible use.

## Criteria by dimension

| Dimension | For a 2 | For a 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual understanding | Links the risks to the origin of biases or to lack of oversight. | Distinguishes risks of fairness, transparency and accountability and ties them to the case. |
| Clarity and structure | The parts are complete and the conditions are clear. | A colleague could apply the conditions without further explanation. |
| Verification and rigor | Contrasts the assistant's analysis with their own judgment and corrects at least one point. | Tests the case with different profiles or scenarios and records what was observed. |
| Ethics and risks | Identifies affected people and concrete, not generic, risks. | Considers those who have no voice in the decision and proposes a protective measure. |
| Applicability | The conditions can be put into practice in their work. | States how compliance would be checked and by whom. |
