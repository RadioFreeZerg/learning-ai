# How to start the course

You do not need to install anything, code or pay. You only need a chat with an AI assistant (a free plan works) and a place to keep notes.

## Your first session, in 4 steps

1. **Open the first lesson:** [L0 · Welcome](curriculum/en/l00-welcome.md).
2. **Copy the prompt.** Scroll down to the section "Prompt for your assistant". It is a gray box of text. Hover over it and press the copy button in its top-right corner (or select all the text and copy it).
3. **Paste it into a new chat** with your AI assistant and send it. The tutor starts on its own: it gives you a privacy notice, explains the course and runs a short interview.
4. **Save what it gives you at the end.** The tutor finishes with a *continuity card* (a short summary). Copy it into your notes: you will need it next time.

## The following sessions

1. Open a **new chat**.
2. Open your current lesson from the [course map](COURSE.md) and copy its prompt.
3. Paste it into the chat and, where it says `[PASTE HERE]`, paste your continuity card.
4. When you finish, save the new card in place of the old one.

When the tutor says it is time for an exercise, do the same with the exercise's prompt: it is in the section "Review prompt for your assistant".

## Three important things

- **Always copy the lesson's prompt.** Do not ask the assistant to "find the course" or give it a web address: many assistants cannot open it and make the lessons up.
- **Do not paste real data about other people.** Replace names and details with placeholders such as `[PERSON A]`.
- **Nothing blocks you.** If an exercise does not pass in two attempts, you choose: repeat it or move on with a warning you can revisit later.

## If something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The tutor does not remember the last session | That is normal. Paste your continuity card. |
| I lost the card | Tell the tutor which lesson you were on and continue from there. |
| The chat gets very long or free messages run out | Ask: "give me the continuity card". Continue later or in another assistant. |
| The tutor explains something different from the lesson | Open a new chat and paste the lesson's prompt again. |
| I disagree with a review | Paste the same prompt and the same submission into another assistant and compare. |

More detail: [`docs/no-file-access.md`](docs/no-file-access.md).
