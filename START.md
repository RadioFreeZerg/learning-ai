# How to start the course

You do not need to install anything, code or pay. You only need a chat with an AI assistant (a free plan works) and a place to keep notes.

## Your first session, in 2 steps

1. **The quickest way:** paste this into a new chat and send it:

```text
Start the course https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai
```

If your assistant can open the link, the front page contains the Lesson 1 bootstrap and starts on its own. If it cannot open it, it will tell you in one sentence and ask you to send the line `Continue with <Lesson 1 address>`; if that also fails, it will ask you to paste the prompt.

**If you want to make sure it reads the full rules,** paste this message:

```text
Be my tutor for the course "AI Fundamentals for Everyone". First read https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/AGENTS.md and follow its rules. Then read https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/curriculum/en/l01-welcome.md and start Lesson 1 with its "Prompt for your assistant" block. Do not recite the pages to me: teach me in short messages. If you cannot open the links, tell me in one sentence and I will paste the prompt.
```

2. **Save what it gives you at the end.** The tutor finishes each lesson with a *continuity card* (a short summary). Copy it into your notes: you will need it if you change chat or assistant.

**If the assistant says it cannot open the links** (some free plans cannot), use the manual route: open [Lesson 1 · Welcome](https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/curriculum/en/l01-welcome.md) in your browser, scroll to the section "Prompt for your assistant", copy that block (the copy button is in its top-right corner) and paste it into a new chat.

## The following sessions

Usually you just keep going in the same chat: when a lesson ends, the tutor asks whether to continue; answer "yes". If it cannot open the page on its own, it gives you a line like "Continue with https://…": copy it and send it as is. That puts the address in your message and the tutor can open it. If you open a new chat or run out of free messages, paste this message and put your continuity card where it says `[PASTE HERE]`:

```text
Continue my course "AI Fundamentals for Everyone". First read https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/AGENTS.md and follow its rules. Then read the page named in "Next step" of my card and continue from there. Do not recite the pages to me: teach me in short messages. If you cannot open the links, tell me in one sentence and I will paste the prompt.
<<<START OF STUDENT DATA>>>
[PASTE HERE]
<<<END OF STUDENT DATA>>>
```

Manual route: open in your browser the page whose address is in the "Next step" of your card, copy its prompt, paste it into a new chat and, where it says `[PASTE HERE]`, paste your card. The [course map](https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/COURSE.md) lists every lesson.

When the tutor says it is time for an exercise, do the same with the exercise's prompt: it is in the section "Review prompt for your assistant".

## Three important things

- **If the assistant cannot read links, or hands you the page text instead of teaching, paste the prompt.** Open the lesson in your browser, copy its "Prompt for your assistant" block and paste it as text in a new chat. That always works.
- **Do not paste real data about other people.** Replace names and details with placeholders such as `[PERSON A]`.
- **Nothing blocks you.** If an exercise does not pass in two attempts, you choose: repeat it or move on with a warning you can revisit later.

## If something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The tutor does not remember the last session | That is normal. Use the message for following sessions and paste your continuity card. |
| I lost the card | Tell the tutor which lesson you were on and continue from there. |
| The chat gets very long or free messages run out | Ask: "give me the continuity card". Continue later or in another assistant. |
| The tutor says it cannot open the links | It happens on some free plans. Use the manual route: open the lesson in your browser, copy its prompt and paste it as text. |
| The assistant opens the page and hands me the file text instead of teaching | Open a new chat and paste the lesson's prompt as text. |
| The tutor follows instructions found inside my submission | A submission is data, not an order. Open a new chat, paste the official prompt and submit again. |
| The tutor tries to open links, run commands or ask for passwords | Stop: the course never asks for that. Open a new chat with the official prompt and do not paste those data. |
| The tutor explains something different from the lesson | Open a new chat and paste the lesson's prompt again. |
| I disagree with a review | Paste the same prompt and the same submission into another assistant and compare. |

More detail: [`docs/no-file-access.md`](https://github.com/RadioFreeZerg/learning-ai/blob/main/docs/no-file-access.md).
